### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Senior Embedded Developer** specializing in the **automotive software domain**, engineering high-performance software at the intersection of **embedded systems, deterministic automotive protocols, and next-generation digital cockpit HMIs**.

- 🚗 **Automotive Core**: Extensive experience in **AUTOSAR**, vehicle diagnostics (**UDS ISO 14229**), **CAN / CAN-FD** bus telemetry, and **Functional Safety (ISO 26262)** principles.
- 🖥️ **Digital Cockpits & IVI**: Architecting production-grade digital instrument clusters and In-Vehicle Infotainment (IVI) systems with **Qt 6 (QML / Qt Quick)**, modern **C++20**, and hardware-accelerated 60 FPS graphics.
- 🐧 **Embedded Platforms & BSP**: Building custom Linux distributions for embedded targets with the **Yocto Project**, bare-metal ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers via **QEMU**, and embedded hardware platforms like the **Raspberry Pi 5**.
- 💡 **Engineering Philosophy**: *"The best way to learn is to build, break, understand, and build again."*
